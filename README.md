# Prof. Dr. Adnan Kavak

**Professor of Computer Engineering at Kocaeli University**

Adnan Kavak leads the Wireless Informatics and Intelligent Systems (WINS) Research Center at Kocaeli University. His research spans wireless communications, computer networks, and artificial intelligence, with applications in digital health. He served as Head of the Department of Computer Engineering from 2013 to 2015.

## Research interests

- Smart antennas, MIMO systems, and next-generation wireless networks
- Wireless sensor networks and the Internet of Things
- AI-assisted diabetes management, remote patient monitoring, and telemedicine

## Education and industry experience

| Degree | Institution | Year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering | The University of Texas at Austin | 2000 |
| M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering | The University of Texas at Austin | 1996 |
| B.S. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering | Middle East Technical University | 1992 |

His doctoral research examined radio propagation for smart antenna systems. Before joining Kocaeli University in 2001, he worked as a satellite control engineer at the PTT Satellite Communications Center and as a senior research engineer at Samsung Telecommunications America, where he worked on adaptive antenna arrays and CDMA base stations. He is a named inventor on four granted U.S. patents.

## Build and preview

Use Node.js 22. Run `npm ci`, then `npm run build` to generate the complete static site in `out/`. Run `npm run preview` to serve that directory locally. The shared templates render at build time. The browser receives HTML, Bootstrap and site CSS, and plain JavaScript for interactive controls.

The GitHub Actions workflow deploys `out/` on pushes to `main`. In the repository’s GitHub Pages settings, use **GitHub Actions** as the source. The workflow supplies the public URL and path prefix automatically.
